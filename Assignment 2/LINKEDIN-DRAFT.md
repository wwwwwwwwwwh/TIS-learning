For SECP1513 Technology and Information System at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, our group prepared an A4 poster on **From Developer to CEO: The Perfect Blend of Technology and Business**, delivered by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Hj. Mohamad Hafis Izran bin Ishak on 26 October 2026.

Using MyREDS as an example, the talk connected software development with customer needs and the responsibilities of running a technology business. My main takeaway is that a useful product needs a complete workflow and dependable support after launch.

My preparation and review focused on organising the content, refining the poster's readability, checking the assignment instructions and improving source attribution. Fitting the material into one page was a useful exercise in deciding what readers need to understand first.

For future projects, I would confirm requirements earlier, test workflows with users and consider maintenance costs from the start.
