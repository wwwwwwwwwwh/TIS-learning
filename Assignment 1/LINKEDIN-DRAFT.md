*Draft for Wong Wen Hao to review. It has not been published.*

For our Technology and Information System assignment at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, our group created a video about innovations explored during our NALI 2026 visit.

My selected project was the AI Japanese Learning Assistant. I interviewed the project team about personalised learning, future development, the role of teachers and the handling of student data. I also developed my AI avatar introduction and reflection with AI assistance, and helped organise and review the combined group video.

My main takeaway was that immediate feedback is useful only when it helps a learner understand and correct a mistake. Personalising practice also does not automatically mean understanding a student's motivation or frustration. These questions connected TIS concepts to practical choices about usability, feedback accuracy and running costs.

The production process taught me to confirm interviewee details early and to check recording quality before leaving a booth. Next time, I would use a shared recording checklist and reserve more time for a full subtitle review.

Thank you to the project teams and to my teammates: Chong Zi Jian, Abdullah Faiyaz Rawoot, Muhammad Aqil, Ahmed Khalid Ahmed and Mouaamn Alali.
